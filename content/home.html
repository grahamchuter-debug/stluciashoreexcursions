Cruise Port Information

Planning Your St Lucia
Cruise Port Day

Most ships dock at Pointe Seraphine or Castries terminals, both within walking distance of downtown shops and taxis. Soufrière and Pitons tours require a winding coastal drive (roughly 1–1.5 hours each way), so early departures and realistic timing matter.

  • ✔ Confirm pier assignment on your ship's daily program
  • ✔ Book Pitons or Soufrière tours early on busy port days
  • ✔ Allow 60–90 minutes buffer before all aboard

Cruise FAQ

St Lucia Shore Excursion Questions

How much time do I need in St Lucia port? ›

Most ships are in port 7–10 hours. Full day Soufrière and Pitons tours use most of that time; catamaran cruises and shorter north island tours suit tighter schedules. Confirm return times when booking.

Can I see the Pitons on a cruise day? ›

Yes. Pitons tours from the cruise port are among the most popular St Lucia shore excursions. Land tours drive to viewpoints near Soufrière; catamaran cruises offer water level views. See our Pitons volcano tours guide.

Is it safe to book independent St Lucia cruise excursions? ›

Licensed local operators run thousands of cruise port tours annually. Choose established providers with clear pickup points, reviews and stated return policies. This site is a planning guide, so always verify details before payment.

What currency is used in St Lucia? ›

The Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD) is official; US dollars are widely accepted near the cruise port. Cards work at many shops and restaurants in Castries.
