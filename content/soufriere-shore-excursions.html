Soufrière, St Lucia's Adventure Capital

Soufrière shore excursions pack the island's greatest hits into one cruise day: the drive in volcano at Sulphur Springs, Diamond Falls Botanical Gardens, Pitons viewpoints and snorkelling in calm bays with the peaks as backdrop.

Tours from Castries or Pointe Seraphine use the west coast road, scenic but winding. Reputable operators build in return to ship buffers.
