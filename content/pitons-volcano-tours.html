Pitons Tours From Cruise Port

The Pitons — Gros Piton and Petit Piton — are UNESCO World Heritage peaks and the defining image of St Lucia. Pitons volcano tours from the cruise port combine viewpoints near Soufrière, coastal villages, rainforest scenery and photo stops with operators who understand ship schedules.

These are among the most requested St Lucia cruise excursions; book early when multiple ships visit on the same day.

Tour Styles

Land Viewpoint Tours

Minibus or private vehicle to Pitons lookout points and Soufrière highlights.

Catamaran Pitons Views

See the peaks from the water on a catamaran cruise.

Custom route with private St Lucia tours.
