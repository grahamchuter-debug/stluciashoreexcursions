St Lucia Cruise Port Overview

Cruise ships typically dock at Pointe Seraphine or Castries Cruise Terminal in the capital. Both are close to downtown Castries, taxis and tour pickups. Some itineraries anchor and tender — check your ship's program the night before.

The island's headline attractions — Soufrière, the Pitons, Sulphur Springs and west-coast snorkel sites — lie on the southwest coast, roughly 45–75 minutes by road from port depending on traffic.

Timing for Shore Excursions

  • Full-day southwest: Depart 8:00–8:30am; return by 3:00–4:00pm typical.
  • Catamaran half-day: Often 9:00am–1:30pm from northern marinas.
  • Buffer: Be back at pier 60–90 minutes before all aboard.
